Right now, everything feels exhausting at work. Why, you might ask. The reason is the AI tools that have entered the workplace. It feels like the expectation of CEO's and upper management is that those tools will accelerate and squeeze every little bit out of every employee. But the pace is crushing, and we as humans are unable to adapt and adopt to the breathtaking pace that those tools bring to the workplace. My gut feeling tells me, that these AI tools are here to stay and they are getting better every minute. At least it feels like that. But how do we humans stay sane in this ever changing world? How can we leverage the new power to really help us doing our work better. But what remains of our work? What is is worth in a world of cheap coding agents? Let's take a look and find ways to cope with this and don't loose interest or fun doing what we have enjoyed doing in the past: developing great software solutions and put them to work. Where is the fun in this highly automated world?
